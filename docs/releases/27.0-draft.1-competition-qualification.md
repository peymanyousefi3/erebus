## Competition coverage

All 14 bundled worlds; two- and four-wheel robots; camera-free, custom-camera and full-sensor models; retained-model recovery; ordinary local, IPC and TCP controllers.

Battery drain from measured wheel motion, swamp drain, natural depletion, pause behavior, and continuous cyan-tile replacement at both 5 and 10 seconds.

80 valid victim/target reports across five scoring fields, exact scoring boundaries and penalties, map symbols and rotations, exit awards, duplicate protection, match timing and replay exports.

Two sensor-driven endurance matches reached 480 simulated seconds, one local and one remote TCP. On this Mac both received 476 game-info replies and ended at 33.12% battery. Movement was checked independently from physical positions.

## Organizer settings retained

ChangeBatteryTime = 5 seconds by default; change the supervisor-owned sensorEnergyConfig.txt and restart to use 10 seconds or another positive duration.

Motor reference: 6.28 rad/s and 0.02 battery percentage points per 16 ms at full speed. Passive and sensor rates retain the supplied zero values. SwampMultiplier = 5 remains configurable.

The draft does not prescribe battery calibration or a swamp multiplier. The existing explicit mapping formula remains in use where the draft prose conflicts with its stated formula.

## Verification limits

Endurance navigation is not an autonomous victim-recognition or mapping solver; scoring and mapping were verified separately with controlled fixtures. The 600-second wall-clock cutoff was tested at its timer boundary rather than by waiting ten minutes.

TCP tests used separate controller processes on the same host. These are functionality checks, not speed benchmarks or a guarantee for every controller or hardware configuration. Software OpenGL was used on hosted Windows/Linux. The Mac installation retains its pre-existing startup-port repair; no Webots installation was changed by this work.

No platform produced simulator ERROR lines. Mac reload batches recorded test-controller termination warnings; the Linux software-rendering host reported a minimum-requirements warning. Administrative judging remains with the referee.
